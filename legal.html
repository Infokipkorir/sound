Data Collection & Fraud Prevention

SoundsCare collects specific data to ensure Platform safety, protect our users, and reduce fraudulent activity. This section explains what we collect for these purposes and why.

IP Address Collection

We collect your Internet Protocol (IP) address to:

  • Detect geographic anomalies and suspicious login patterns
  • Prevent account takeover and unauthorized access
  • Identify and block fraudulent registrations, including use of proxies or VPNs that mask true location during identity-sensitive processes
  • Investigate reported disputes and policy violations
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
SECURITY MEASURE

Geolocation Tracking

For workers, we collect:

  • GPS Coordinates: Precise location during registration and, where relevant, work assignments
  • Regional Data: Country and region information
  • Movement Patterns: Travel history between assignments, only with your consent

This helps us:

  • Verify you are in a supported operating region
  • Match you with nearby job opportunities
  • Prevent location-based fraud
  • Support user safety in the event of an emergency or dispute

Device Information Collection

We collect device identifiers including:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system version
  • Browser type and version
  • Mobile network information, where accessible

This helps prevent:

  • Creation of multiple fraudulent accounts from the same device
  • Bot and automated fraud attempts
  • Credential sharing across accounts
  • Spoofed identity registrations
Consent Required: During registration, you are asked to consent to relevant data collection described here. You can withdraw consent for non-essential collection at any time via your account settings or by contacting us, though this may limit your access to certain features (for example, location-based job matching).

Fraud Detection

Our systems, including automated checks and manual review by our trust & safety team, look for patterns such as:

  • Suspicious registration patterns
  • Velocity fraud (multiple registrations from the same source in a short time)
  • Account takeover attempts
  • Payment fraud and chargebacks
  • Fabricated or manipulated credential documents

Data Minimization

We collect only the data reasonably necessary for the stated purposes. We do not collect:

  • Biometric data (such as fingerprints or facial recognition templates) without your explicit, separate consent
  • Health or medical information, except where voluntarily provided or required for a specific verified role
  • Financial account details beyond what is necessary for payment processing
  • Criminal background data, except where performed with your consent as part of an optional verification service

Data Access & Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the data we've collected about you
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Opt out of non-essential data collection
