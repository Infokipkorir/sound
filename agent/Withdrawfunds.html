How earnings are verified

  • ValidA job-post earning is valid to withdraw once that job is closed and a worker was hired through SoundsCare. Hire commissions are valid as soon as the hire is confirmed.
  • PendingThe job is still open. The earning stays in your wallet and becomes withdrawable once the job closes with a hire.
  • Not validThe job closed without a SoundsCare hire. That earning is deducted from your wallet when you submit a withdrawal.
