Raw WebRTC peer-to-peer needs a TURN server for calls to connect reliably across most home/mobile networks (STUN alone fails whenever both users are behind restrictive NAT — common on Safaricom/Airtel mobile data). Enter your TURN credentials once you have a server (e.g. coturn on a small VPS, or Twilio/Metered's free TURN tier).
