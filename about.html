One umbrella, every skill

Skilled or unskilled, this gate doesn't turn you away.

We don't sort people into tiers — we sort jobs by what needs doing. Guards and gardeners stand on the same platform as chefs and house managers, because the goal was never to rank people, it was to get everyone hired.

What you need here isn't a certificate. It's a sharp mind and a genuine will to earn — the rest, we'll help you prove.

Vetted, all the way home

Before anyone reaches your gate, we've already reached theirs.

We verify ID, next-of-kin and home-area references for every worker on the platform — down to the village. It's a lower-risk way to hire: if a placement ever needs a second look, we know exactly where to go.

"Your household's peace is our daily business peace."

What "verified" means here

Identity confirmed, next-of-kin recorded, home-area references checked. A worker isn't just a profile to us — they're someone we can always find again, and someone employers can always trust again.

Where we're headed

Vision, mission, and the goal we're walking toward.

Vision

An East Africa where no job is too small to be dignified, and no one has to walk all day just to be seen.

Mission

Put every job — skilled and unskilled — under one roof, matched by capability and verified by character, so hiring is safer and being hired is fairer.
