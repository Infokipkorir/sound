How it works

How to hire house help or find a domestic job in 3 steps

Simple for families, free for workers.

  1. Create your free account

    Employers and workers sign up with phone OTP verification. Workers build a profile with skills, experience and the area they work from.

  2. Search and shortlist

    Employers browse verified helpers by role, county or city and neighbourhood. Workers browse and apply to jobs near them.

  3. Connect and agree terms

    Pay a one-time KES 300 connection fee with M-Pesa or Airtel Money to contact the helper, then agree on pay, duties and start date directly.
